BEFORE SENDING
--------------
Replace every {{PLACEHOLDER}} with accurate, personalised details. Address a
specific person where possible and explain briefly why Myntix may be relevant
to their role or school. Do not send the template unchanged to a bulk list.

The logo images are loaded from:
https://myntix.com/brand/myntix-lockup.png
https://myntix.com/brand/myntix-wordmark.png

Confirm those URLs are publicly available over HTTPS before sending. Some
email clients block images until the recipient chooses to display them, so the
email must still make sense with images disabled.

AUSTRALIAN EMAIL OUTREACH
-------------------------
Promotional email is generally a commercial electronic message. Australian
spam rules require an appropriate consent basis before sending; the email must
identify the sender, include contact details, and provide a clear, functional
unsubscribe method. A publicly listed work email is not blanket permission;
any inferred consent should be relevant to the recipient's role and the
message. Keep a suppression list and honour opt-out requests promptly. Check
current ACMA guidance or obtain legal advice if unsure.

Official guidance: https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam

The template's reply-with-unsubscribe line is intended to be monitored. Keep
the sender mailbox active and process opt-out requests. Do not use these files
as justification to send unsolicited bulk email.
